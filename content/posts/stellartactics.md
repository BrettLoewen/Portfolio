With _Stellar Tactics_, I wanted to recreate the core gameplay from one of my favourite games, _XCOM 2_. _XCOM_ is a turn-based tactics game where the player commands a squad of soldiers in a fight against alien invaders. The player must carefully move their soldiers across the battlefield to find and accomplish an objective, eliminating any alien threats as they go. This is the gameplay that I wanted to make in this prototype.

I decided that soldiers needed to perform 3 key actions:

-   Move across the battlefield
-   Fight enemies
-   Interact with the environment

My goal was to have a player command a squad of soldiers with these actions against a computer-controlled enemy squad in a simple level. After launching the game and clicking Play, the player is presented with their squad. Before they can fight, they must move a soldier to the door and click on it using the interact action to open it and access the hallway where 2 enemies wait. The player must defeat the enemies using their attack actions: shoot, melee, and grenade. After opening the final door, the player must defeat a second group of enemies, at which point the level ends and the player can either replay it or return to the menu.

With this scenario fully implemented, I accomplished my goal of recreating the core gameplay of _XCOM 2_ with this small, focused prototype.
