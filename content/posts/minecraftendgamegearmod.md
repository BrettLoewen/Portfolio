After years of playing [Minecraft](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/about-minecraft) and thinking about hypothetical mods that I would enjoy playing, I decided to finally make my own. I followed [this tutorial series](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLKGarocXCE1H_HxOYihQMq0mlpqiUJj4L) by Youtuber Modding by Kaupenjoe to build my first Minecraft mod.

My mod, _Endgame Gear_, adds 2 new materials for armor, weapons, and tools to the game: Prismarine and Dragonite. Prismarine can be found in tuff pillars at the bottom of the Overworld's deepest oceans, while Dragonite can be found in asteroid-like geodes floating in the skies of the End dimension. Both these materials are designed to mimic the endgame material Netherite, where mining the ore gives scraps that the player can craft into ingots and combine with diamond gear on a smithing table to produce the upgraded item.

However, both new materials have additional uses beyond Netherite. Prismarine ingots can be used to craft the previously uncraftable throwable trident and a full set of Prismarine armor allows the player to breathe underwater. Dragonite can be used to craft rockets that function like fireworks for glider speed boosts but have durability instead of being instantly consumed, and the Dragonite chestplate has a built-in elytra glider.

These new materials and the gear they can create expands the endgame and gives players more reason to continue with a Minecraft world after beating the End Dragon.
