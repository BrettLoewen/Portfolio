When I was learning Unity and game development, I completed a Unity Learn course about building a game called _Tanks_ where 2 players drive tanks in an arena and the last one standing wins. I enjoyed the course and liked the game idea, so several years later I revisited the course and expanded the project into a more complete game. I added more levels, support for up to 4 players, and tank customization. I also overhauled the tank controls and used one-hit-kill to make the game more fast-paced and engaging.
